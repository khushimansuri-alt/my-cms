Our Story

Three Decades of Shaping Confident Learners

Founded in 1993, TreeHouse High School began with one classroom and one belief: every child can grow into a confident, caring individual when given the right environment.

Today our family of 7 schools serves more than 10,000 students, guided by 450+ dedicated teachers.
