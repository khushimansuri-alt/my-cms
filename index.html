Our Common FAQ's

What are the admission criteria?

Admissions are open from Nursery to Class XII. Please fill the admission enquiry form or call our helpline and our team will guide you through the process.

Is toilet training required before enrollment?

Our caring staff supports young children, and we work with parents to make the transition to school comfortable.

What safety measures do you have in place?

The campus has 24x7 security, CCTV monitoring, trained staff and strict visitor management.

Do you celebrate birthdays or special events?

Yes. We celebrate festivals, birthdays and special days so that children learn and enjoy together.

What is the student-to-teacher ratio?

We keep classes balanced so every child receives personal attention from teachers.
